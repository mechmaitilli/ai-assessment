## 2. The 88 kg Reading: Not Fat, and the Arithmetic Says So

The scale reads exactly the 25 July starting weight. **This is not a regain, and no part of the plan should be changed on the strength of it.**

### The arithmetic

To store 2.5 kg of body fat requires a surplus of roughly **19,000 kcal**, which across the five days since the last weigh-in is about **3,800 kcal per day above maintenance**. Maintenance is 2565 to 2735.

Logged intake across that window:

| Day | Calories | Band | Variance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sat 25 Jul | ~2350 | 2565 to 2735 | −215 |
| Sun 26 Jul | ~2400 | 2565 to 2735 | −165 |
| Mon 27 Jul | ~2535 | 2565 to 2735 | −30 |
| Tue 28 Jul | ~2377 | 2565 to 2735 | −188 |
| Wed 29 Jul | ~2555 | 2565 to 2735 | −10 |
| | | **Cumulative** | **−608 kcal** |

**Every single day ran a deficit.** The required surplus is not merely absent, the sign is wrong. Across the whole window the cumulative balance is **608 kcal negative**, which is about 0.08 kg of fat *lost*. Fat gain of 2.5 kg is arithmetically impossible on this intake. Whatever the scale is measuring, it is not stored energy — and the body composition almost certainly moved slightly in the intended direction while the number on the scale moved 2.5 kg the other way.

### What the 2.5 kg actually is

1. **Retained stool.** Constipation since 26 July with one small-volume movement on 27 July. Several days of accumulation plausibly accounts for **0.5 to 1.5 kg** on its own, and it is the one component the athlete can literally feel.
2. **Glycogen and its bound water.** The diet break raised carbohydrate from cut levels. Every gram of stored glycogen binds roughly 3 g of water, so refilling muscle and liver glycogen adds **1 to 1.5 kg** over a few days. This is the intended effect of a diet break, not a side effect.
3. **Sodium load.** Yesterday was a grill day: tannour at 700 mg sodium per 100 g, fattoush, muttabal, kafta, plus Pepsi Zero. High sodium against low fluid holds water.
4. **Creatine.** Ongoing intracellular water retention, roughly **1 kg**, already documented as the explanation for the top-of-range creatinine.
5. **Training fluid.** Yesterday's shoulder session took two exercises to failure. Inflammation and fluid shift into worked muscle for 24 to 72 hours after.

These overlap rather than stacking cleanly, but the combined range comfortably contains 2.5 kg without a single gram of fat.

### Decision

- **Macros hold at the 85.5 kg calibration.** No recalculation on this number.
- **The standing "recalculate at 83.5 kg" item is suspended**, not deleted. It assumed the next move would be downward. The trigger is now a *confirmed* 2 kg move in either direction, and 88.0 kg does not qualify because it is measured against active constipation.
- **Re-weigh on three consecutive mornings once the constipation clears, and average them.** That average is the real number. A single morning weight taken mid-constipation is not a body composition measurement.
- **Do not cut. Do not add cardio. Do not shorten the diet break.** Reacting to water and stool with a calorie reduction is how a diet break gets wasted.
