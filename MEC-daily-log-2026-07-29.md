### The issue: two exercises taken to failure

The plan called for 2 RIR throughout. The lateral raise went to 0 RIR on all three sets and the reverse pec deck on sets two and three.

**Why this matters more than usual right now:**

1. **Accessory volume was deliberately trimmed by one set to manage fatigue.** Training the remaining sets to failure restores the fatigue that trimming was meant to remove, during a diet break whose whole purpose is recovery.
2. **On lateral raises with rotator cuff history, the final two reps before failure are where form degrades** and the shoulder loads in the position the programme exists to avoid. This is the one exercise where 0 RIR is an injury risk rather than only a fatigue cost.
3. The session's purpose was calibration. Numbers produced at failure are not 2 RIR numbers, so they cannot be used as targets.

No pain resulted. Do not repeat it.

### Next shoulder session: Wednesday 5 August

**Two loads go down. That is not regression, it is correcting failure-numbers back to 2 RIR numbers.**

| # | Exercise | Target | Change | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|---|
