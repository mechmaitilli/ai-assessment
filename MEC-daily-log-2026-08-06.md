## 2. GI: THE DAY-THREE READ IS IN, AND IT OVERSHOT

**Reported: almost no movement — very small pieces, heavy pushing, and a persistent sense that the gut is full and needs to move.**

|---|---|

| Had to push a lot | Straining on nearly every passage |

**Point for point, today matches the picture the log opened with — the one that predates the bars coming out.**

### The three-day arc, completed

| Day | Bar-free day | Read |
|---|---|---|
| 4 August | 1 | Swooshy AM, two formed pieces PM |
| 5 August | 2 | Half formed, loose at the end |
| **6 August** | **3** | **Small hard pieces, straining, incomplete** |

**The trend did not level off at normal. It went loose → half-formed → hard in three days.**

### What the test actually answered

**The bar test has read out, one day early and more sharply than designed:**

1. **The polyols were driving the looseness.** Consistency swung from swooshy to hard within three days of removal — that speed is the signature of an osmotic agent leaving the system, not of a fibre adaptation.
2. **Underneath, the baseline gut is constipation-prone.** The bars were not just causing loose stool — **they were accidentally treating the hard-stool tendency at the same time**, by pulling water into the colon every day. Remove them and the late-July pattern resurfaces almost immediately.

**Both things were true at once.** The 26–36 g daily polyol load overshot: it turned a constipation-prone gut into a loose one. Removing it swung the pendulum straight back.

**His 2 August comment — "I feel got is full always" — now reads as part of this picture**, present even during the loose phase.

### What changes today

**START PSYLLIUM TODAY, not Friday.** The only reason it was deferred was to keep the bar test clean, and the test has now answered. There is nothing left to confound.

- **Dose: start at 1 teaspoon (~5 g) in a full glass of water, once today**, ideally with a meal. Ramp toward twice daily over the next week if tolerated
- **Water is non-negotiable with it.** Psyllium without fluid makes hard stool worse. Yesterday's ~2.5 L is the number to repeat, and in this heat it matters double
- **Do not strain.** Pushing hard against small hard stool is how haemorrhoids and fissures happen. If it does not come, stand up and try later — the urge returns with the gastrocolic reflex after meals
- **Figs and walnuts stay.** Their fibre is now working with the plan, not against a test
- **Bars stay out.** The answer to hard stool is fibre and water, not reintroducing a 30 g polyol load
- **Magnesium bisglycinate stays for sleep.** It is not the tool for this — but if psyllium plus water has not moved things in 3–4 days, **magnesium citrate at night is the correct next lever**, and that decision goes through Dr Chalhoub

### What does not change

**No alarm.** Yesterday produced one well-relieved passage; today's poor one reflects a single transition day, not a week of failure. The fibre base (~21 g yesterday, from real food) is the best it has been, and psyllium starts on top of it today. **Give the combination 3–4 days before judging it.**
