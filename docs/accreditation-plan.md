# Accreditation Plan for AI Training Programs

*Prepared July 2026 — for adding recognized accreditation to AI training certificates.*

---

## 1. The reality check (read this first)

There are **three different things** people mean by "accredited," and they have very
different paths:

| What you want on the certificate | Who grants it | Realistic? |
|---|---|---|
| "CPD Accredited" / CEU credits | CPD bodies (UK), IACET (US/global) | ✅ Yes — fastest route |
| "Microsoft training partner" / prepares for Microsoft Certification | Microsoft AI Cloud Partner Program | ✅ Yes — via partner + MCT route |
| "Built on Anthropic's AI Fluency framework" / Claude Partner | Anthropic Claude Partner Network | ✅ Yes — free to join |
| "Accredited BY Anthropic" or "Accredited BY Microsoft" | — | ❌ Neither company accredits third-party certificates directly |

**Important:** Anthropic and Microsoft do not "accredit" external training programs the
way an accreditation body does. What they offer is **partnership, licensing of official
content, and their own certifications that your students can earn**. Your certificate
can truthfully say things like *"Delivered by a Microsoft Training Services Partner"*
or *"Curriculum based on Anthropic's AI Fluency framework"* — but never *"Accredited by
Microsoft/Anthropic"* unless they grant that wording in a partner agreement.

## 2. Track A — Fast credibility: CPD / CEU accreditation (1–3 months)

This is the quickest way to put a recognized accreditation mark on your certificates.

### Option A1: CPD accreditation (UK-based, globally recognized)
- **Bodies:** The CPD Certification Service (cpduk.co.uk), CPD Standards Office, The CPD Group.
- **Process:** Free consultation → register as a CPD provider → submit course materials
  (slides, learner outcomes, assessments) → assessment in ~30 days → certified status +
  trustmark for your certificates.
- **Cost:** roughly **£200–£1,000 per course** (package deals available for multiple courses).
- **What you get:** "CPD Accredited" logo on certificates, listing in CPD directories,
  CPD hours/points printed on each certificate.

### Option A2: IACET accreditation (US/international, issues CEUs)
- **Standard:** ANSI/IACET 1-2018 Standard for Continuing Education and Training.
- **Eligibility:** legally registered business, **at least 1 year in operation**, running
  the training as described for at least 3 months.
- **Process:** purchase Standard + application (**$495 to start**; full accreditation
  costs more), rigorous review of your instructional design, assessment, and record-keeping.
  Reaccreditation every 5 years.
- **What you get:** authority to award **CEUs** (widely accepted by US employers,
  government agencies, and professional licensing boards).

**Recommendation:** Start with **CPD (A1)** — cheaper, faster, and internationally
recognizable. Add IACET later if you target US corporate/government clients.

## 3. Track B — Vendor alignment: Anthropic and Microsoft (2–9 months)

### B1. Anthropic — Claude Partner Network + AI Fluency

1. **Join the Claude Partner Network** (free, any organization bringing Claude to market
   is eligible). Anthropic committed $100M in 2026 to partner training, technical support,
   and co-marketing. Includes access to the Partner Portal and Anthropic Academy materials.
2. **Use the AI Fluency curriculum legally.** Anthropic's *AI Fluency: Framework &
   Foundations* and the *Teaching AI Fluency* (train-the-trainer) course are released
   under a **Creative Commons license** — you can adapt and deliver them in your own
   program, with attribution per the license terms.
3. **Get your trainers Claude-certified.** Anthropic runs an official certification
   program through **Pearson VUE** (e.g., Claude Certified Architect, with Practitioner /
   Architect / Developer role tracks rolling out through 2026). Certifications count
   toward partner program standing.
4. **Pursue the Services Track** of the Claude Partner Network once established — this is
   the closest thing to an Anthropic "stamp" for a services/training firm.

**Certificate wording you can earn:** *"Member of the Claude Partner Network"*,
*"Curriculum adapted from Anthropic's AI Fluency framework (CC-licensed)"*,
*"Taught by Claude Certified [Architect/Practitioner] instructors."*

### B2. Microsoft — Training Services Partner + MCT

1. **Join the Microsoft AI Cloud Partner Program** (free base membership via Partner Center).
2. **Get at least one trainer certified as a Microsoft Certified Trainer (MCT):**
   - Hold a qualifying Microsoft certification (AI/Copilot foundational requirement,
     e.g., AB-730/AB-731 as of 2026),
   - Complete the MCT Onboarding Experience (~3-day program),
   - Deliver at least one Microsoft course per program year; renew annually.
3. **Enroll for the Training Services Partner designation** in Partner Center. Requirements
   include using **official Microsoft Courseware via Microsoft Learn**, delivering all
   courses with MCTs, issuing post-training achievement codes to every student, and
   running the Microsoft quality survey (Metrics that Matter).
4. **Deliver official Microsoft AI courses** — e.g., AI-900 (Azure AI Fundamentals),
   AI-102 (Azure AI Engineer), and the Copilot/AI applied-skills courses — so your
   students walk away with **Microsoft-issued credentials** alongside your certificate.

**Certificate wording you can earn:** *"Delivered by a Microsoft Training Services
Partner"*, *"Prepares for Microsoft Certified: Azure AI Fundamentals (AI-900)."*

## 4. Track C — Formal/institutional accreditation (6–18 months, optional)

For long-term positioning, depending on where you operate:

- **ISO 21001** (Educational Organizations Management System) — international,
  certifiable by any accredited certification body; strong signal for B2B/government.
- **National regulated-qualification routes** — e.g., endorsement by an Ofqual-regulated
  awarding organization in the UK (NCFE, TQUK, Highfield), state/provincial licensing for
  private training providers in the US/Canada, or the national training authority where
  you operate (e.g., KHDA in Dubai, HRD Corp in Malaysia). These require a quality
  management system, assessor policies, and internal verification.
- **University partnership** — co-certify with a university's continuing-education arm;
  slower but carries academic weight.

## 5. Recommended 12-month roadmap

| Phase | When | Actions | Cost (est.) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Foundations | Month 1 | Document each course: learning outcomes, syllabus, assessment method, trainer CVs, feedback process. This package is required by every body below. | Time only |
| 2. CPD accreditation | Months 1–3 | Apply to The CPD Certification Service; submit flagship course first. | £200–£1,000/course |
| 3. Anthropic alignment | Months 1–3 | Join Claude Partner Network (free); adapt AI Fluency CC content; book Pearson VUE Claude certification for lead trainer. | Exam fees only |
| 4. Digital credentials | Months 2–4 | Issue certificates as verifiable digital badges (Credly/Accredible) showing CPD hours + partner logos per each program's brand rules. | ~$1–3k/yr platform |
| 5. Microsoft partner | Months 3–9 | Join AI Cloud Partner Program; lead trainer earns MCT; enroll as Training Services Partner; add AI-900 as a course offering. | Cert exams + MCT fee |
| 6. Scale up | Months 9–18 | Evaluate IACET (if US-focused) or ISO 21001 / national accreditation (if institutional clients demand it). | $5k–$20k |

## 6. Compliance notes for your certificates

- Never print "Accredited by Anthropic" or "Accredited by Microsoft" — use the partner
  wording each program's brand guidelines permit.
- CPD/IACET marks may only appear on the specific courses that were assessed.
- Anthropic's CC-licensed AI Fluency content requires attribution; check the specific
  CC variant (BY / BY-NC / BY-SA) before charging for derived courses.
- Keep attendance and assessment records — every accreditor audits these at renewal.
