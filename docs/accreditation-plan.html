01 The reality check

"Accredited" means three different things, each with a very different path. Getting this straight first saves months.

What you want on the certificateWho grants itRealistic?
"CPD Accredited" / CEU creditsCPD bodies (UK), IACET (US/global)Yes — fastest
Microsoft training partner / prepares for Microsoft certsMicrosoft AI Cloud Partner ProgramYes
Built on Anthropic's AI Fluency framework / Claude PartnerAnthropic Claude Partner NetworkYes — free
"Accredited by Anthropic" or "by Microsoft"—No
The one thing to understand Anthropic and Microsoft do not "accredit" third-party training the way an accreditation body does. They offer partnership, licensed content, and their own certifications your students can earn. Your certificate can truthfully say "Delivered by a Microsoft Training Services Partner" or "Curriculum based on Anthropic's AI Fluency framework" — but never "Accredited by Microsoft/Anthropic" unless a partner agreement grants that exact wording.

02 Track A — CPD / CEU accreditation

The quickest way to a recognized accreditation mark on your certificates. Start here.

1–3 months · recommended first

Option A1 · CPD accreditation (UK-based, globally recognized)

  • Bodies: The CPD Certification Service, CPD Standards Office, The CPD Group.
  • Process: free consultation → register as a CPD provider → submit course materials → assessed in ~30 days → certified status + trustmark.
  • Cost: roughly £200–£1,000 per course (package deals for multiples).
  • You get: "CPD Accredited" logo, directory listing, CPD hours printed on each certificate.
US / international · issues CEUs

Option A2 · IACET accreditation

  • Standard: ANSI/IACET 1-2018 for Continuing Education and Training.
  • Eligibility: legally registered business, at least 1 year in operation, running the training as described for 3+ months.
  • Cost: $495 to start (Standard + application); full accreditation costs more. Reaccredit every 5 years.
  • You get: authority to award CEUs — accepted by US employers, government, and licensing boards.
Recommendation Start with CPD (A1) — cheaper, faster, internationally recognizable. Add IACET later only if you target US corporate or government clients.

03 Track B — Anthropic & Microsoft

Vendor alignment. Not "accreditation" — but powerful, credible wording your certificate can carry, plus real credentials your students earn.

Free to join

B1 · Anthropic — Claude Partner Network + AI Fluency

  • Join the Claude Partner Network (free; open to any org bringing Claude to market). Backed by a $100M 2026 commitment to partner training and co-marketing.
  • Use the AI Fluency curriculum legally. Framework & Foundations and the Teaching AI Fluency (train-the-trainer) course are released under a Creative Commons license — adapt and deliver them with attribution.
  • Certify your trainers via Anthropic's Pearson VUE program (Practitioner / Architect / Developer tracks rolling out through 2026).
  • Pursue the Services Track once established — the closest thing to an Anthropic "stamp" for a training firm.
Certificate wording you can earn: "Member of the Claude Partner Network" · "Curriculum adapted from Anthropic's AI Fluency framework" · "Taught by Claude Certified instructors."
2–9 months

B2 · Microsoft — Training Services Partner + MCT

  • Join the Microsoft AI Cloud Partner Program (free base membership via Partner Center).
  • Certify a trainer as MCT: hold the AI/Copilot foundational cert (AB-730/731), complete the ~3-day MCT onboarding, deliver ≥1 Microsoft course/year, renew annually.
  • Enroll for the Training Services Partner designation: use official Microsoft courseware via Microsoft Learn, deliver with MCTs, issue achievement codes + quality survey.
  • Deliver official courses (AI-900, AI-102, Copilot skills) so students earn Microsoft-issued credentials alongside your certificate.
Certificate wording you can earn: "Delivered by a Microsoft Training Services Partner" · "Prepares for Microsoft Certified: Azure AI Fundamentals (AI-900)."

04 Track C — Institutional (optional)

Slower, heavier, for long-term B2B / government positioning — 6–18 months.

  • ISO 21001 (Educational Organizations Management System) — international, certifiable by any accredited body; strong B2B/government signal.
  • National regulated-qualification routes — e.g. an Ofqual-regulated awarding organization (NCFE, TQUK, Highfield) in the UK, state/provincial private-provider licensing in the US/Canada, or your national training authority (KHDA in Dubai, HRD Corp in Malaysia).
  • University partnership — co-certify with a university's continuing-education arm; slow but academically weighty.

06 Certificate compliance notes

  • Never print "Accredited by Anthropic" or "Accredited by Microsoft" — use only the partner wording each brand's guidelines permit.
  • CPD / IACET marks may appear only on the specific courses that were assessed.
  • Anthropic's CC-licensed AI Fluency content requires attribution — check the exact CC variant (BY / BY-NC / BY-SA) before charging for derived courses.
  • Keep attendance and assessment records — every accreditor audits these at renewal.
