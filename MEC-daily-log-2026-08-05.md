### 3b. GI — IMPROVING, NOT RESOLVED

**Day 2 of the bar removal.**

| Date | Passages | Consistency |
|---|---|---|
| 31 July | **3** | — |
| 3 August | 2 | **Swooshy throughout**, both |
| 4 August | 2 | Swooshy AM, **two formed pieces** PM |
| **5 August** | **1, morning only** | **Half formed, loose at the end** |

**Both variables are moving the same way: frequency falling 3 → 2 → 1 while consistency firms.** That is the direction of travel expected from removing an osmotic irritant.

**One morning passage with good relief and no straining is the target state, not a shortfall.** Normal frequency runs from three times a day to three times a week. Three in one day on 31 July was the outlier — that reading is what killed the slow-transit hypothesis, because it fails the frequency criterion for functional constipation outright.

**Formed-first, loose-last is itself informative.** The leading portion has spent longer in the colon with more water absorbed; the tail is more recent material moving faster than it should. As transit slows toward normal, the loose fraction shrinks from the back. **That is what partial resolution looks like.**

**Intake explains part of today's single passage independently.** The day ran to roughly 18:00 on ~220 kcal. There was almost nothing in the system to move, and the gastrocolic reflex needs a meal to fire.
