## 11. Bloodwork Panel to Request

### Tier 1: most likely to explain the symptoms

| Test | Why |
|---|---|
| **TSH, Free T4, Free T3** | An underactive thyroid produces exactly this triad: fatigue, low appetite, constipation. Most important. |
| **CBC** | Anaemia, infection, general screen |
| **Ferritin + serum iron, TIBC, transferrin saturation** | Iron deficiency causes fatigue long before anaemia shows on a CBC |
| **Vitamin B12 + folate** | Especially relevant: no red meat and no fish, the two richest B12 sources |
| **Vitamin D (25-OH)** | Commonly low in Beirut indoor lifestyles |

### Tier 2: worth having at 49

| Test | Why |
|---|---|
| Total testosterone, free testosterone, SHBG | Age-relevant. Prolonged deficits suppress it. **Draw 07:00-10:00.** |
| Fasting glucose + HbA1c | Metabolic baseline |
| Lipid panel | Cardiovascular baseline |
| ALT, AST, GGT | Liver baseline, relevant with high protein intake |
| Creatinine, urea, eGFR | Kidney baseline. See the creatine note below. |
| Sodium, potassium, calcium, magnesium | Cramps, fatigue, bowel motility |
| CRP | Non-specific inflammation |

### Four things to tell the doctor
1. **"I take creatine monohydrate."** It raises serum creatinine and can make kidney function look impaired when it is not.
2. **"I have been in a deliberate caloric deficit and lost 2.5 kg."** Context for thyroid and testosterone results.
3. **"I eat no red meat and no fish."** Why B12, iron and omega-3 status should be checked rather than assumed.
4. **Symptoms in one line:** constant fatigue for several weeks, low appetite, constipation, but training performance still strong and progressing.

### Practical
- Go **fasted**, 8 to 12 hours, water only.
- Go **early**, 07:00 to 10:00, for a valid testosterone reading.
- **Do not train that morning.** Hard exercise the day before can shift CK, liver enzymes and testosterone.
- If only a few can be run, the priority order is: **thyroid, ferritin, B12, vitamin D, CBC.**

## 13. Meal Ideas That Worked

### Karisheh cheesecake bowl
200 g karisheh, 1 tbsp honey or 1 stevia sachet, 2 chopped dates, generous cinnamon, 1 banana.
**~26 g protein, 63 g carbs, 4.5 g fat, ~400 kcal.**

**Method:** whip the karisheh with a fork or blender for 30 seconds until smooth. This is the step that turns it from curd into cheesecake and is the single biggest upgrade. Fold in the sweetener and half the cinnamon. Layer with sliced banana, scatter the dates, dust the rest of the cinnamon. Chill 10 minutes.

**Variations:** 1 tsp instant coffee stirred in (ahweh cheesecake), 1 tbsp cocoa powder (chocolate), orange blossom or rose water, or freeze 20-25 minutes to eat like ice cream.

### Lazy man'oushe
1 tannour or saj, 150 g karisheh, za'atar, 1 tsp olive oil, tomato, cucumber, mint. Fold and eat cold, or 2 minutes in a dry pan.
**~31 g protein, 70 g carbs, 4.5 g fat, ~470 kcal.**

**Sweet version:** karisheh with 1 tbsp honey instead of za'atar and vegetables. Traditional and equally good.

### Batch tawook
1 kg raw marinated tawook, scraped of excess marinade, cooked on a rack at 200°C for 20-25 minutes (or air fryer 190°C for 15 minutes). Portion into 6 containers of ~165 g raw (~120 g cooked), each **~51 g protein and ~310 kcal.** Three days in the fridge, freeze the rest.

**Weigh raw, not cooked.** 150 g raw becomes 110-120 g cooked, and that difference is where most chicken tracking goes wrong.
