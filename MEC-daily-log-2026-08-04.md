**Both moved between 2 and 3 August, and they moved in opposite directions:**

**Fibre went down and the stool got looser. That is evidence against fibre as the driver.**

**One day of data, and the two are not cleanly separable in a controlled sense** — but the directions disagree, and only one of them tracks the outcome. **The confounding introduced yesterday has accidentally produced a useful natural experiment.**

**Either outcome is informative. This is the first genuinely clean test in the investigation.**
